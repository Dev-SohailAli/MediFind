# Task 44 Support and Contributor Capacity Governance Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Prepare safe support and contributor capacity decisions as volume grows while preserving founder accountability, least privilege, privacy, non-emergency boundaries and existing-record integrity.

**Architecture:** A capacity and training matrix covers support categories, hours, service limits, escalation and handoff. A separate role/access register covers individual accounts, MFA, minimum permissions, confidentiality, onboarding/offboarding, review and revocation evidence. Thresholds can cap or pause new activation without disabling safe search or corrupting existing records.

**Tech Stack:** Existing `apps/web`, approved support routes and static guidance, role/access policies, synthetic support cases, audit records and redacted queue metrics. No shared credential, medical-advice workflow, prescription outsourcing, 24/7 promise, WhatsApp channel, staffing contract, new provider, automation or access grant is added.

**Spec:** `docs/claude-tasks/stewardship/task-44-support-contributor-capacity.md`, `docs/business-and-commercial.md`, `docs/pilot-operations.md`, `docs/account-recovery-runbook.md`, `docs/audit-log-policy.md`, `docs/staff-access-lifecycle-policy.md`, `docs/repository-security-and-delivery.md`, and accepted Tasks 30 and 37 evidence.

## Global Constraints

- Do not begin until a founder-approved capacity problem, named support owner(s), current incident/metric evidence and employment/contractor/confidentiality/training/access review are available.
- Use synthetic support cases and aggregate/redacted metrics only. No private personnel data, prescriptions, OTPs, tokens, raw contacts or medical advice enters the matrix or evidence.
- Every person uses an individual account with least privilege, MFA where required, audited actions and explicit expiry/revocation. Shared credentials and informal admin access remain prohibited.
- Support is non-emergency and non-clinical. Pharmacy reviewers retain pharmacy/professional decisions; MediFind does not certify prescriptions or provide medical advice.
- A capacity recommendation may continue, cap, pause or prepare. It does not grant a role, commit staffing, create a contract or authorize sensitive workflow expansion.

## Task 1: Define support coverage and capacity thresholds

**Files:**

- Create: support category, hours, escalation, handoff and capacity matrix
- Create: aggregate queue and threshold register
- Modify: pilot operations/support guidance and task brief with accepted outcome
- Read: current incident, support, pilot, metric and founder-accountability evidence

- [ ] **Step 1: Map service boundaries**

Document account/access, translation, accessibility, listing-quality and technical support categories; Fiji business hours; pharmacy direct-contact boundaries; emergency/clinical exclusions; security escalation; and safe status/fallback behavior.

- [ ] **Step 2: Define thresholds and actions**

Use approved evidence to set review triggers for queue age, verification delay, listing freshness, incident load, response quality and owner availability. For each threshold specify continue, cap, pause or prepare, affected workflow, safe search behavior and re-open criteria.

- [ ] **Step 3: Define handoff evidence**

Require category, opaque case ID, severity, owner, next action, deadline, escalation decision and redacted audit reference. Never copy raw prescription, token, contact or sensitive support text into a handoff.

## Task 2: Define training and contributor role controls

**Files:**

- Create: role/scope/training/access/revocation matrix
- Create: synthetic onboarding, transfer and departure checklists
- Modify: staff access and contributor guidance only after owner review

- [ ] **Step 1: Set minimum training**

Cover privacy and anti-enumeration, phishing and MFA/recovery, listing quality, translation and accessibility, incident response, prescription boundaries, redaction and escalation. Record version, reviewer and completion evidence without private personnel data.

- [ ] **Step 2: Map least-privilege roles**

For each proposed support or contributor role record purpose, permitted metadata/actions, branch scope, prohibited data, owner, approver, expiry/review date, MFA requirement and audit expectations. Keep prescription content and break-glass access outside routine support.

- [ ] **Step 3: Rehearse lifecycle events**

Exercise synthetic invitation, MFA setup/recovery, branch-isolated handoff, role downgrade, immediate departure revocation and stale-session invalidation. Confirm access removal is auditable and does not alter existing records.

## Task 3: Verify escalation, privacy and continuity

**Files:**

- Create: synthetic support/escalation/revocation result set
- Modify: capacity decision record and unresolved-risk register

- [ ] **Step 1: Exercise representative cases**

Rehearse account compromise, suspected prescription exposure, suspicious link/impersonation, unsafe upload, translation/accessibility issue, listing-quality report and pharmacy handoff. Verify severity, safe messaging, anti-enumeration and escalation path.

- [ ] **Step 2: Verify branch and role isolation**

Confirm support cannot infer inaccessible records, cross branch boundaries, read prescription content or bypass owner/pharmacy decisions. Check MFA, audit redaction, offline/error behavior and revocation during handoff.

- [ ] **Step 3: Decide capacity posture**

Present aggregate evidence and residual risks to the founder/support owner. Recommend continue, cap, pause or prepare; document what must be corrected before any future role or cohort expansion.

# Task 45 Annual and Renewed Public-Readiness Review Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce a dated, evidence-backed continue/cap/pause/prepare/exit recommendation after Tasks 38–44 without treating the review as automatic public, paid or protected-data authorization.

**Architecture:** A founder/release-owner packet joins the current operator/legal, privacy, security/recovery, accessibility/language, support/capacity, cost, continuity and source-governance records. Each area has owner, source, review date, evidence class, independence note, status, residual risk and stop condition. Material gaps block only the affected capability and remain visible.

**Tech Stack:** Existing repository Markdown, redacted evidence registers, local synthetic fixtures and approved independent review artifacts. No production deployment, external service, credential, data export, public announcement, provider/binding or new release surface is added.

**Spec:** `docs/claude-tasks/stewardship/task-45-annual-public-readiness-review.md`, the governance roadmap, Tasks 36–44, `docs/decisions.md`, `docs/pilot-operations.md`, `docs/incident-response-runbook.md` and the active web-only Cloudflare architecture.

## Global Constraints

- Require accepted or explicitly closed Tasks 38–44, current named owners, current legal/privacy status, and independent review where the gate requires it.
- Do not infer legal compliance, public readiness, certification, production health, vendor approval, cost affordability or translation quality from missing evidence.
- Use synthetic or redacted evidence only. Never include prescriptions, raw contacts, credentials, tokens, private personnel data or unnecessary identifiers.
- Preserve the active web-only Cloudflare direction and all approved data, retention, support, accessibility, recovery, cost and release boundaries.
- The recommendation cannot grant access, activate a cohort, publish a notice, sign a contract, change an ADR or authorize deployment.

## Task 1: Assemble the evidence matrix

**Files:**

- Create: annual/renewed-readiness evidence matrix
- Read: Tasks 36–44 evidence, current ADRs, policies, incident/recovery and pilot records
- Modify: task brief and roadmap with source links and outcome

- [ ] **Step 1: Define review areas**

Cover operator/legal identity, privacy rights/retention, support/disclosure, accessibility/localization, security/recovery, source governance, contributor capacity, continuity, cost and protected-data gates.

- [ ] **Step 2: Classify evidence**

For each area record source, owner, last/next review, status, evidence class (local synthetic, protected or hosted/public), reviewer independence, unresolved risk and stop condition.

- [ ] **Step 3: Check freshness and conflicts**

Identify expired reviews, missing owners, broken links, conflicting ADR/policy language, unverified public claims and any provider/data/cost/retention change. Do not silently repair policy meaning.

## Task 2: Assess capability-specific readiness

**Files:**

- Create: gap and capability-impact register
- Modify: release decision record and unresolved-risk register

- [ ] **Step 1: Apply hard stops**

Block affected capability for critical legal/privacy, authorization, prescription safety, recovery, accessibility, support-capacity or cost-control gaps. Record scope, owner, remediation and retest requirement.

- [ ] **Step 2: Separate evidence boundaries**

Confirm local synthetic rehearsal is not hosted evidence, a protected test is not a public claim, and an independent report is not a deployment approval. Keep raw protected evidence out of the packet.

- [ ] **Step 3: Reconfirm current direction**

Verify the review does not authorize native apps, Firebase/GCP, a second backend, unsupported providers, direct browser bindings, analytics/session replay, chat, delivery, clinical advice or broader data collection.

## Task 3: Produce the decision and next-review plan

**Files:**

- Create: founder/release-owner decision packet and next-review calendar
- Modify: decision log/roadmap status only after human acceptance

- [ ] **Step 1: Recommend posture**

Present continue current scope, cap growth, pause capability, prepare a separately approved decision request or orderly exit. Include residual risks, owners and prerequisites.

- [ ] **Step 2: Set earlier-review triggers**

Record incident, owner/provider, legal advice, cost, data-scope, accessibility, support-capacity, recovery or public-claim changes that require review before the annual date.

- [ ] **Step 3: Obtain explicit human decision**

Capture decision-maker, date, scope, conditions and rejected/ deferred options. Do not treat silence, a passing checklist or an agent-authored packet as approval.

## Task 4: Verify and hand off

**Files:**

- Create: redacted evidence matrix, unresolved-risk register and decision record
- Modify: task brief, stewardship README, docs index and roadmap

- [ ] **Step 1: Run checks**

Run formatting and link/structure checks; run only approved local/synthetic or independent review commands. Do not claim hosted, production, public-readiness or certification results without evidence.

- [ ] **Step 2: Preserve stop conditions**

Ensure unresolved critical gaps remain visible and the packet contains no secrets, real records, private personnel data or new authority.

- [ ] **Step 3: Commit the review packet**

Commit `docs: prepare annual public-readiness review`; schedule the next review and leave future capability changes as separate decision requests.

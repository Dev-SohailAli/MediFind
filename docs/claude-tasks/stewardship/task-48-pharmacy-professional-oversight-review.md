# Task 48: Pharmacy-professional oversight and protected-workflow review

## Goal

Reconfirm that pharmacy verification, responsible-person evidence, staff
training, prescription review and sensitive-medicine boundaries remain under
appropriate pharmacy/professional ownership before any protected workflow or
cohort expansion continues.

Prepared implementation plan: [Task 48 pharmacy-professional oversight review](../../superpowers/plans/2026-08-18-task-48-pharmacy-professional-oversight-review-implementation.md).

## Gate

Requires accepted Tasks 16, 30, 36, 38, 42, 45 and 47, current Fiji
pharmacy/legal review, named qualified pharmacy reviewer(s), current pharmacy
agreements and training versions, and a founder/release-owner decision.

## Scope

- Review responsible-pharmacist, licensing, business, branch and official-
  contact evidence requirements, expiry/renewal handling and suspension state.
- Reconfirm role boundaries for inventory managers, prescription reviewers,
  owners, verifiers/admins and support; pharmacy professionals retain
  prescription validity and dispensing decisions.
- Review required training/attestation versions for prescription boundaries,
  privacy, MFA, listing quality, accessibility/language, incidents and
  escalation, including retraining triggers.
- Maintain the documented exclusion/hold position for controlled, restricted or
  otherwise legally sensitive medicines until a specific approved policy exists.
- Rehearse synthetic reviewer loss, branch isolation, unsafe/ambiguous request,
  verification expiry, break-glass escalation and neutral pending-request
  handling; never use real prescriptions or make a clinical decision.

## Out of scope

MediFind dispensing or prescribing, clinical advice, professional licensing
decisions, automatic verification, outsourced prescription review, changing
medicine legality, real pharmacy onboarding, real credentials, public cohort
activation, or a new clinical/registry provider.

## Acceptance

- Each protected workflow has a named pharmacy/professional owner, verified
  role boundary, evidence freshness, training version, escalation path and
  stop condition.
- Expired, missing or disputed evidence disables the affected public listing,
  prescription or reservation capability without rerouting or exposing a
  pending request; safe non-sensitive search remains where verified.
- Synthetic exercises prove branch isolation, reviewer-loss behavior, MFA and
  revocation, anti-enumeration, audit redaction and neutral buyer messaging.
- The result recommends continue, cap, pause, prepare or seek professional/legal
  decision; it does not grant access or certify a pharmacy or medicine.

## Verification and handoff

Run document/link checks, training/role matrix checks and approved synthetic
protected-workflow rehearsals. Attach redacted evidence and unresolved-risk
register. Commit:
`chore: review pharmacy professional oversight boundaries`

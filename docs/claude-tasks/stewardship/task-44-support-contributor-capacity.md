# Task 44: Support and contributor capacity governance

## Goal

Scale human operations safely when pharmacy, buyer, support or contributor
volume grows, while preserving founder accountability, least privilege and the
non-emergency service boundary.

Prepared implementation plan: [Task 44 support and contributor capacity governance](../../superpowers/plans/2026-08-18-task-44-support-contributor-capacity-implementation.md).

## Gate

Requires Tasks 30 and 37, a founder-approved capacity problem, named support
owner(s), current incident/metric evidence and a review of employment,
contractor, confidentiality, training and access obligations.

## Scope

- Define support categories, hours, escalation, handoff, coverage, service
  limits and non-emergency/clinical boundaries for any expanded team.
- Map minimum training for privacy, phishing, MFA/recovery, listing quality,
  translation, accessibility, incident response and prescription boundaries.
- Define role-based access, individual accounts, MFA, onboarding/offboarding,
  access review, confidentiality and audit requirements for contributors or
  contractors.
- Establish capacity thresholds that pause cohort growth or sensitive
  workflows before support quality or safety degrades.
- Rehearse synthetic support escalation and departure/access-revocation paths.

## Out of scope

Shared credentials, informal admin access, outsourced prescription review,
medical advice, 24/7 support promises, WhatsApp support, staffing contracts,
employee onboarding or role grants without separate approval.

## Acceptance

- Every support/contributor role has scope, owner, training, access, hours,
  escalation and revocation evidence.
- Capacity thresholds can pause new activation while preserving safe search and
  existing-record integrity.
- Synthetic cases prove anti-enumeration, redaction, branch isolation, MFA and
  immediate revocation during handoff/departure.
- The output recommends continue, cap, pause or prepare; it does not grant
  access or commit the founder to a staffing model.

## Verification and handoff

Run quality checks, role/access tests and synthetic support/revocation
rehearsals. Attach the capacity and training matrix without private personnel
data. Commit:
`docs: define safe support and contributor capacity controls`

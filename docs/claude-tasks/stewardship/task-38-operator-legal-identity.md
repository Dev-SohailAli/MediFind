# Task 38: Operator, legal identity and obligations

## Goal

Keep MediFind's operator identity, public notices, pharmacy agreements,
commercial posture, liability, insurance and tax/registration assumptions
current before any material external operation.

## Gate

Requires Tasks 30-37, a named Fiji legal reviewer, founder decision on the
operating structure and current advice on registration, tax, contracts,
liability, insurance and privacy-controller obligations. No legal identity may
be guessed or published by this task.

## Scope

- Build an owner-approved checklist of operator identity, contact method,
  business address, governing law, dispute handling and registration
  disclosures needed for the current service state.
- Map which notices, agreements, privacy terms, support copy and domain/email
  records must change when the operating structure changes.
- Record paid-operation triggers: subscriptions, payment data, employees,
  investors, material contracts, broader pharmacy commitments and insurance.
- Define review/expiry dates, evidence owner and a safe stop path when legal
  advice is pending.

## Out of scope

Incorporation filing, legal advice, contract signing, public publishing,
payment collection, insurance purchase, founder name disclosure and changes to
the product's service boundary.

## Acceptance

- The record identifies the current approved operator or explicitly blocks
  external activation until one is approved.
- Public notices and agreements have a versioned dependency list and no
  placeholder identity where law requires a controller/contracting party.
- Paid-operation triggers and required legal/business reviews are explicit.
- No personal or confidential legal correspondence enters the repository.

## Verification and handoff

Run document/link checks and any copy-completeness checks. Attach only a
redacted decision matrix, reviewers, dates and unresolved blockers. Commit:
`docs: record operator identity and legal readiness gates`

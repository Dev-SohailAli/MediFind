# Free pilot pharmacy agreement: required terms

## Status and purpose

This is a documentation draft for the free Suva pilot, not legal advice and not a signed contract. Fiji legal review must convert it into the final form before a pharmacy is activated. The final agreement must identify the legally valid MediFind operator without naming the founder publicly unless the founder chooses to do so. An authorised pharmacy owner may accept the final agreement in the app; the system must retain auditable evidence of the accepted version and acceptance event.

The pilot has no subscription fee for participating pharmacies or buyers. It does not create paid placement, a commission on medicine sales, payment processing, delivery or exclusivity.

## Required terms

1. **Participant and branch.** Identify the verified pharmacy legal entity/owner, authorised contact, participating branch or branches, responsible pharmacist/reviewer and registered Fiji address.
2. **MediFind role.** MediFind provides discovery, selected-pharmacy prescription routing and reservation-request tooling only. It does not prescribe, diagnose, sell, dispense, process payment, deliver, determine prescription validity or guarantee a sale.
3. **Pharmacy responsibility.** The pharmacy is responsible for its legal authority, licensing, staff supervision, medicine eligibility, listing accuracy, price/tax/charge disclosure, professional prescription review, dispensing decision, collection, records and regulatory obligations.
4. **Listing operations.** Named staff keep public availability, FJD price, pack/form/strength and hours current at least once each business day. Staff must not publish exact stock quantities, controlled/restricted products or a prescription-required listing without an active authorised reviewer.
5. **Prescription and reservation workflow.** The pharmacy alone receives a buyer-selected prescription and may approve, reject or allow it to expire. It must use only named individual accounts, keep MFA enabled, protect its devices, review quarantine flags through its professional process and never treat MediFind technical checks as a clinical/legal legitimacy decision. Reservation approval is pharmacy-controlled, includes pickup instructions/expiry and never guarantees dispensing.
6. **Staff and access.** The pharmacy owner invites, approves and revokes staff access; access is branch-scoped and must be removed promptly when employment/authority ends. The pharmacy promptly reports suspected compromise, misrouted data, incorrect listings or other security/privacy events through the approved channel.
7. **Data and confidentiality.** Each party uses personal/prescription data only for the agreed workflow, limits access to authorised people, follows the final privacy/retention policy and cooperates with access, deletion, incident and regulator requests as legally required. The pharmacy must not export or reuse MediFind buyer data for unrelated marketing.
8. **Pilot operations.** Pilot is invite-only, Suva branches only, and founder-operated support is Monday-Friday, 9:00am-5:00pm Fiji time. MediFind may pause features, remove listings or suspend access for safety, security, compliance, stale data, misuse or operational reasons; it will give practical notice where safe.
9. **Feedback.** The pharmacy permits MediFind to use de-identified operational feedback to improve the pilot. Marketing testimonials, logos or public announcements require separate written approval.
10. **End of pilot.** Either party may withdraw under the final agreement. On withdrawal/suspension, MediFind removes public discovery and revokes staff access; pending prescriptions are never forwarded, and buyers receive a safe status notice. Data handling follows the legally approved retention schedule.

## Before signature/activation checklist

- Fiji legal/pharmacy review completed and final agreement accepted in-app by an authorised pharmacy representative, with versioned auditable evidence.
- Pharmacy verification evidence and participating branch verified.
- Named owner, reviewers and inventory staff invited; privileged MFA set up.
- Pharmacy staff completes synthetic-data training for listing refresh, prescription/quarantine, reservations and incident reporting.
- Pharmacy acknowledges the current privacy notice, prescription workflow, support hours and no-guarantee boundary.
- Exact pilot start/end, withdrawal contact, final operator identity and commercial future-terms approach are recorded.

# Public notice and legal-identity requirements

## Decision

MediFind's public-facing privacy notice, terms, support pages and app-store text must not name the founder personally. They may identify the service simply as **MediFind** during documentation and synthetic-data development.

Before any external buyer or pharmacy pilot is activated, establish and use a MediFind business identity. Fiji legal review must confirm the legally valid operator identity, contact method, business address (where required), governing law/dispute information and any registration disclosures required for the actual operating structure. Insert those approved details into the public notices before launch. Do not use a placeholder or anonymous identity where law requires an identifiable controller or contracting party.

## Required public notices before external pilot

- Privacy notice: controller/operator identity and contact, collected data, purposes, legal/consent basis as advised, recipients/processors, hosting/transfer, retention, user rights/requests, security summary and changes.
- Terms of use: service boundary; no diagnosis, prescribing, dispensing, payment or delivery; pharmacy responsibility; no stock/reservation/dispensing guarantee; acceptable use; availability/support; liability wording only after legal review; and governing terms required by counsel.
- Prescription upload notice: selected-pharmacy-only disclosure, pharmacy decision authority, technical checks/quarantine, buyer cancellation-before-open boundary, expiry and approved retention/deletion policy.
- Pharmacy pilot agreement: the signed/accepted agreement in [the pilot agreement](pilot-pharmacy-agreement.md).
- Support and safety notice: non-emergency service, published support hours, emergency guidance and anti-phishing guidance.

All public notices require complete English, iTaukei and Fiji Hindi review. Legal, pharmacy and translation approval is a release gate for public/prescription functions; they are not implementation copy that Claude may invent.

## Provisional privacy position

Until the retention schedule is approved through Fiji legal/pharmacy review, MediFind does not accept real prescriptions. Documentation and testing use only synthetic files. The final notice must give plain-language, specific retention periods for prescription files, review records, reservations, audit records, backups and account deletion outcomes.

# Pharmacy onboarding and training pack

## Purpose

This pack is the minimum operational training for each pilot pharmacy. It supports safe self-service listings and pharmacy-owned prescription/reservation decisions; it does not train staff to provide clinical advice or change their professional obligations.

## Activation sequence

1. **Application and evidence.** The owner submits branch, contact, business/licensing and responsible-person evidence. MediFind verifies it and records the decision.
2. **In-app pilot agreement.** An authorised owner reviews the current agreement, privacy notice and support/safety boundaries in the app, then gives explicit acceptance. The system records agreement version, owner account ID, branch, server timestamp, acceptance text/hash and IP/device/session evidence appropriate to the approved privacy policy. A copy is available to the owner; changed terms require new acceptance before affected use continues.
3. **Access setup.** The owner completes verified personal-email sign-in and authenticator-app MFA, then invites only named staff by phone. Every privileged invitee completes email verification and MFA before access. The owner assigns roles and immediately revokes departing staff.
4. **Synthetic walkthrough.** Staff complete the role-relevant exercises below using fake data only. No live pharmacy is activated before completion is recorded.
5. **Readiness review.** MediFind checks successful training, current branch hours, a test OTC listing, a test prescription-required listing (only if an active reviewer exists), notification receipt, the owner’s acknowledgment and pilot scope. Only then can a verified Suva branch become public.

## Training modules

| Module | Audience | Required outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MediFind boundary and support | All owner/staff | Understand MediFind is discovery/routing/reservation tooling, not a prescriber, seller, dispenser, payment service or emergency/clinical support; know published founder support hours. |
| Account safety and MFA | Owner/staff | Use individual accounts, verified personal email and authenticator MFA; never share accounts/codes; recognise phishing; report a lost device or suspected compromise promptly. |
| Listings and freshness | Owner/inventory manager | Create eligible medicine listings with correct identity, form, pack, strength, OTC/prescription status, FJD price and availability; no exact stock quantity; refresh daily; manage hours and stale warnings. |
| Prescription review | Owner/reviewer | Open only branch-scoped assigned requests behind fresh biometric/MFA gate; understand selected-pharmacy-only routing, generic notifications, technical quarantine labels and two-business-day unviewed expiry; make the professional validity/dispensing decision. |
| Reservations and collection | Owner/reviewer | Approve/decline accurately, set feasible pickup instruction/expiry, disclose price change before approval, cancel after approval only with operational reason, mark collection accurately and handle buyer cancellation/no-show. |
| Privacy, incidents and escalation | All owner/staff | Use minimum data, avoid exports/screenshots/unapproved communication, report suspicious access or misrouted data immediately, and use the documented pharmacy/MediFind escalation path. |

## Mandatory synthetic exercises

- Create, correct and refresh a fake OTC listing; show how a 24-hour stale warning and seven-day removal occur.
- Create a fake prescription-required listing and verify that it is not public without an active authorised reviewer.
- Receive a generic test notification; enter the in-app queue, complete MFA/biometric gate and review a safe fake prescription.
- Handle one fake normal prescription, one fake quarantine-labelled prescription and one expired request. Confirm that technical flags do not decide legitimacy.
- Approve, decline, expire and cancel fake reservations; verify price disclosure, pickup window and the buyer notice for each relevant state.
- Revoke a fictional staff member, report a fictional phishing message and locate the emergency escalation/support contacts.

## Records and refreshers

MediFind records only the training module/version, learner account, branch, completion/attestation time, failures needing follow-up and retraining due date. Do not record quiz answers containing health data. Retrain when the prescription/reservation workflow, security policy, agreement or role meaning materially changes, and at least annually for active staff.

## Go-live checklist

- Verified branch and authorised owner; required agreement accepted in-app.
- Owner/reviewers have MFA and no shared accounts; minimum one active reviewer for prescription-required workflows.
- Listing-quality and request/reservation synthetic exercises completed.
- Pharmacy confirms daily listing-refresh process, branch hours, price responsibility and pickup process.
- Emergency/security/escalation contacts verified; pharmacy understands that public launch is Suva-only and invite-only.

# Product brief

## Problem and outcome

People in Fiji may know the medicine they need but not which nearby pharmacy has it. MediFind will make current, pharmacy-supplied availability and listed FJD prices discoverable in one responsive web experience that can be installed to a device Home Screen. The v1 pilot is limited to verified pharmacies in Suva.

## Audiences

- **Buyer:** registered person seeking a medicine and, when required, a prescription review and collection reservation.
- **Pharmacy staff:** authorised staff who maintain their pharmacy's details and listings, review prescriptions, and decide reservations.
- **Pharmacy owner:** controls staff access and is accountable for submitted business information.
- **MediFind verifier/admin:** verifies pharmacy eligibility, handles reports and support, and audits platform activity. MediFind does not make dispensing decisions.

## Goals and measures

- Make a searched, currently available medicine easy to find at a Suva pharmacy.
- Keep public availability and price useful: measure listing update freshness, successful searches, result-to-pharmacy contacts, prescription response time, and reservation decisions.
- Onboard and verify pilot pharmacies safely: measure completion, verification turnaround, active staff, and listing quality.

Targets are set with pilot pharmacies before launch; no target is treated as a clinical outcome.

## V1 scope

Registered buyers search medicine listings and view pharmacy name, map/location, opening hours, availability status, listed FJD price and last-updated time in the responsive web app/PWA. They may request an over-the-counter collection reservation directly, or select a verified pharmacy and upload a prescription for review before a prescription-required reservation can be approved. Buyers receive generic web notifications where supported and follow up directly by the pharmacy's published phone number, hours and directions. V1 provides no in-app chat. Pharmacy staff use the same protected web application through their role-specific workspace.

## Out of scope

Payments, delivery, live inventory/POS integrations, exact stock quantities, clinical advice, medicine substitution advice, anonymous access, government-program eligibility decisions, and nationwide expansion are excluded.

## Phased roadmap

1. **Pilot:** Suva, manual listing updates, verified pharmacies, discovery, prescription review, reservations.
2. **Validate:** improve catalog quality, onboarding and notification reliability using pilot evidence.
3. **Expansion candidates:** wider Fiji coverage, integrations, payments, delivery, and government-program information only after separate legal, operational and product decisions.

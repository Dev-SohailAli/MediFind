# Business and commercial model

## Ownership and pilot posture

MediFind is initially founder-funded and operated by its sole proprietor. The pilot is deliberately small: recruit and support two to three Suva pharmacies before considering broader rollout. This operating model means automation, vendor spending, support hours and compliance commitments must remain proportionate to one person's capacity.

## Pricing

The pilot is free for participating verified pharmacies and buyers. MediFind will not charge buyers in v1. After pilot validation, the preferred commercial direction is a simple pharmacy SaaS subscription or listing fee. No subscription, fee, advertising arrangement or commercial relationship may alter medicine-search ranking or result prominence. Pricing, billing provider, contracts, taxes, refunds and any paid plan features are explicitly deferred until the pilot demonstrates sustained pharmacy value and legal/business advice is obtained.

The founder-approved MVP infrastructure ceiling is FJD 50–100 per month, excluding developer-account fees, domains/business email, SMS and separately approved human assurance costs. Use the [free-first, scale-in-place architecture](free-first-production-architecture.md) in every environment. A billed production project may legitimately consume no-cost allowances, but accepting real prescriptions still requires paid Sydney storage/backups and every security/compliance gate; a free tier never authorises weaker controls.

## Legal and operational constraints

The project begins as an individual business rather than an incorporated company. Before accepting paid subscriptions, payment data, investor money, employees, or material contractual commitments, document the applicable Fiji registration, tax, contract, liability, insurance and privacy obligations and decide whether to incorporate.

## Pilot success evidence

The pilot should establish that pharmacies can keep listings current daily, respond within their stated business hours, and see enough buyer interest to justify a later paid service. Feedback sessions with each pilot pharmacy and representative buyers are mandatory inputs to pricing and expansion decisions.
